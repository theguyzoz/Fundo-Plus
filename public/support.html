❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I link my WhatsApp to Fundo Plus? ▼
Go to your Dashboard and click the WhatsApp tab in the sidebar. Click "Generate Link" to get a pairing message, then send that message to our WhatsApp bot number. Your account will be linked automatically within seconds.
Why is Prok AI giving incorrect answers? ▼
Prok AI is powered by a large language model and can occasionally make mistakes — especially with very specific exam questions. Always verify important answers with your textbooks or teacher. You can also rephrase your question or ask for a step-by-step explanation to get a better response.
How many messages can I send per day? ▼
Free accounts get 25 AI chat messages per day, 15 image generations, and 5 PDF exports. Limits reset at midnight UTC. A Pro plan with unlimited access is coming soon.
My account was suspended — what do I do? ▼
If your account was suspended, you can submit an appeal from the login page or contact us using the form below. Please include your email address and a brief explanation of the situation.
How do I delete my account? ▼
You can delete your account from Account Settings (/me). Deletion is permanent and removes all your data. If you have trouble, contact us using the form below.
Can I use Fundo Plus without WhatsApp? ▼
Yes! The web platform (this site) gives you full access to Prok AI, quiz generation, past papers, and all features. WhatsApp linking is optional — it's provided so you can also use the bot from your phone without needing to open a browser.
How do Skills and Syllabus work? ▼
The Skills section in your Dashboard lets the AI understand what subject and level you're studying. Upload or select a syllabus, and Prok AI will tailor its responses to that specific curriculum and topic. You can also use it to create focused project files for deep study sessions.
