2. Description of Service

Fundo Plus is an AI-powered education platform designed for Zimbabwean students studying the ZIMSEC O-Level and A-Level curriculum. Features include:

  • AI study chat (Prok AI)
  • Quiz generation from study material
  • Past papers library
  • WhatsApp integration
  • Document (PDF/Word) generation
  • Community discussion board
  • Skills & syllabus study tools

We collect only the information needed to provide and improve the service: your email or phone, name, school, and usage data. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

You can delete your account at any time from the Account Settings page. Deletion removes your personal data within 30 days.

7. Community Rules

The Fundo Plus Community board is a public space for students to discuss studies, share tips, and support each other. Rules:

  • Be respectful — no personal attacks, discrimination, or hate speech
  • Stay on topic — educational content and study-related discussion only
  • No spam, self-promotion, or commercial content
  • Do not share personal contact information
  • Do not post copyrighted exam content in full

Messages that violate these rules will be removed. Repeat offenders will be banned from the community or the platform.

The community has a rolling limit of 5,000 messages. Older messages are automatically deleted when this limit is reached.
