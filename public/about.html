About Fundo Plus

We're on a mission to make quality education accessible to every Zimbabwean student — no matter where they are.

🎯 Our Mission

Fundo Plus was built to bridge the gap between Zimbabwean students and the quality of education they deserve. By combining AI technology with the ZIMSEC curriculum, we give every student — whether in Harare or a rural village — access to a personal AI tutor, past papers, quiz generation, and more. Available on web and WhatsApp, so it works wherever you have a phone.

📖Our Story

Fundo Plus was created by XTech, a Zimbabwean technology company focused on solving real problems for local communities. We noticed that students studying for ZIMSEC O-Level and A-Level exams often lacked access to resources, experienced tutors, and personalised study help.

We set out to change that. Using the latest AI technology, we built Prok AI — a study assistant that understands the ZIMSEC curriculum deeply. Prok AI can explain complex topics, work through maths problems step by step, generate quizzes, and even create full study documents — all from a simple chat interface.

Because not all students have access to laptops or PCs, we made Prok AI available directly on WhatsApp. All you need is your phone.

⚡Technology

Fundo Plus is powered by state-of-the-art large language models (LLMs) fine-tuned for educational content. Our Prok AI engine understands LaTeX mathematics, renders equations beautifully, and provides worked solutions for ZIMSEC subjects.

We use KaTeX for real-time mathematical typesetting, so when you ask about calculus, trigonometry, or algebra — the answer is clear, precise, and beautifully formatted.

Our platform is built with Node.js on the backend, served from Railway, and designed to be fast, reliable, and accessible even on slower internet connections.

🛠️Built by XTech
