# Key Mathematics Formulae — ZIMSEC O-Level & A-Level

## Algebra
- Quadratic formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
- Difference of squares: $a^2 - b^2 = (a+b)(a-b)$
- Perfect square: $(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$
- Sum of AP: $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(2a + (n-1)d)$
- Sum of GP: $S_n = \frac{a(r^n - 1)}{r - 1}$, $S_\infty = \frac{a}{1-r}$ (|r|<1)

## Geometry
- Area of triangle: $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$ or $A = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$
- Circle area: $A = \pi r^2$, Circumference: $C = 2\pi r$
- Pythagoras: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
- Arc length: $l = \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$
- Sector area: $A = \frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$

## Trigonometry
- SOHCAHTOA: $\sin\theta = \frac{O}{H}$, $\cos\theta = \frac{A}{H}$, $\tan\theta = \frac{O}{A}$
- Sine rule: $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$
- Cosine rule: $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$
- $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$

## Statistics
- Mean: $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum fx}{\sum f}$
- Standard deviation: $\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum f(x-\bar{x})^2}{\sum f}}$
- Probability: $P(A) = \frac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}$

## Calculus (A-Level)
- Derivative of $x^n$: $\frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = nx^{n-1}$
- Chain rule: $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du} \cdot \frac{du}{dx}$
- Product rule: $\frac{d}{dx}(uv) = u\frac{dv}{dx} + v\frac{du}{dx}$
- $\int x^n dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$ (n ≠ -1)
