# ZIMSEC O Level Combined Science — Paper 1 (Multiple Choice)
# 40 Questions | Topics: Biology (Cell biology, Nutrition, Photosynthesis, Respiration,
# Transport, Reproduction, Genetics, Ecology), Chemistry (Atomic structure, Bonding,
# Reactions, Acids & Bases, Electrolysis, Organic), Physics (Force, Energy, Electricity,
# Waves, Measurement, Pressure)

Q : Which organelle is responsible for producing energy (ATP) in a cell?
A: Nucleus  B: Mitochondria  C: Chloroplast  D: Ribosome
ANS: B

Q : The process by which plants make their own food using sunlight is called
A: Respiration  B: Transpiration  C: Photosynthesis  D: Digestion
ANS: C

Q : Which of the following is the correct word equation for aerobic respiration?
A: Glucose + Water → Carbon dioxide + Oxygen  B: Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy  C: Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen  D: Glucose → Lactic acid + Energy
ANS: B

Q : The blood vessel that carries oxygenated blood from the lungs to the heart is the
A: Pulmonary artery  B: Aorta  C: Pulmonary vein  D: Vena cava
ANS: C

Q : In a food chain, organisms that feed on producers are called
A: Decomposers  B: Primary consumers  C: Secondary consumers  D: Apex predators
ANS: B

Q : Which hormone controls blood sugar levels in humans?
A: Adrenaline  B: Oestrogen  C: Insulin  D: Testosterone
ANS: C

Q : In Mendelian genetics, if T represents tall (dominant) and t represents short (recessive), the genotype of a homozygous tall plant is
A: Tt  B: tt  C: TT  D: tT
ANS: C

Q : Which part of the male reproductive system produces sperm?
A: Prostate gland  B: Sperm duct  C: Urethra  D: Testes
ANS: D

Q : The process by which water moves through a plant from roots to leaves is
A: Osmosis  B: Transpiration  C: Diffusion  D: Active transport
ANS: B

Q : Which of the following is a function of the cell membrane?
A: Controlling what enters and leaves the cell  B: Producing proteins  C: Storing genetic material  D: Producing energy
ANS: A

Q : An atom of carbon has atomic number 6. How many electrons does it have?
A: 12  B: 3  C: 6  D: 8
ANS: C

Q : Which type of bond involves the sharing of electrons between atoms?
A: Ionic bond  B: Metallic bond  C: Covalent bond  D: Hydrogen bond
ANS: C

Q : Which of the following is an acid?
A: NaOH  B: HCl  C: NaCl  D: Ca(OH)₂
ANS: B

Q : The pH of pure water is
A: 0  B: 7  C: 14  D: 5
ANS: B

Q : During electrolysis of dilute sulphuric acid, what is produced at the cathode?
A: Oxygen  B: Chlorine  C: Hydrogen  D: Sulphur
ANS: C

Q : Which of the following is a physical change?
A: Burning wood  B: Rusting of iron  C: Melting of ice  D: Digestion of food
ANS: C

Q : Sodium chloride (NaCl) is formed when sodium reacts with chlorine. This is an example of a
A: Decomposition reaction  B: Displacement reaction  C: Synthesis (combination) reaction  D: Reversible reaction
ANS: C

Q : What is the chemical formula for carbon dioxide?
A: CO  B: CO₂  C: C₂O  D: C₂O₃
ANS: B

Q : Which group of elements in the Periodic Table are known as noble gases?
A: Group I  B: Group VII  C: Group O (Group 18)  D: Group II
ANS: C

Q : Organic compounds are those that contain the element
A: Oxygen  B: Nitrogen  C: Carbon  D: Hydrogen
ANS: C

Q : Which of the following is a scalar quantity?
A: Force  B: Velocity  C: Acceleration  D: Speed
ANS: D

Q : A force of 20 N acts on an area of 4 m². What is the pressure?
A: 80 Pa  B: 16 Pa  C: 5 Pa  D: 24 Pa
ANS: C

Q : The SI unit of electric current is the
A: Volt  B: Ohm  C: Ampere  D: Watt
ANS: C

Q : Which law states that the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it?
A: Newton's First Law  B: Ohm's Law  C: Boyle's Law  D: Charles's Law
ANS: B

Q : What type of energy is stored in a stretched rubber band?
A: Kinetic energy  B: Chemical energy  C: Elastic potential energy  D: Thermal energy
ANS: C

Q : The transfer of heat by direct contact between particles is called
A: Convection  B: Radiation  C: Conduction  D: Evaporation
ANS: C

Q : Which of the following correctly defines the principle of moments?
A: A body is in equilibrium when total clockwise moments equal total anticlockwise moments  B: Pressure is force divided by velocity  C: Energy cannot be created or destroyed  D: Every action has an equal and opposite reaction
ANS: A

Q : The speed of light in a vacuum is approximately
A: 3 × 10⁶ m/s  B: 3 × 10⁸ m/s  C: 3 × 10¹⁰ m/s  D: 3 × 10⁴ m/s
ANS: B

Q : What is the relationship between frequency (f) and period (T) of a wave?
A: f = T  B: f = T²  C: f = 1/T  D: f = 2T
ANS: C

Q : A car accelerates from rest to 20 m/s in 5 seconds. What is its acceleration?
A: 100 m/s²  B: 25 m/s²  C: 4 m/s²  D: 0.25 m/s²
ANS: C

Q : The process by which a liquid changes to a gas below its boiling point is called
A: Condensation  B: Sublimation  C: Evaporation  D: Boiling
ANS: C

Q : Which environmental factor is NOT required for photosynthesis?
A: Sunlight  B: Water  C: Carbon dioxide  D: Oxygen
ANS: D

Q : What is the function of haemoglobin in red blood cells?
A: Fighting infection  B: Clotting blood  C: Carrying oxygen  D: Producing hormones
ANS: C

Q : A plant cell placed in a concentrated salt solution will
A: Swell and burst  B: Become turgid  C: Undergo plasmolysis (shrink)  D: Stay the same
ANS: C

Q : Which gas is released during photosynthesis?
A: Carbon dioxide  B: Nitrogen  C: Oxygen  D: Hydrogen
ANS: C

Q : The movement of particles from a region of high concentration to low concentration is called
A: Active transport  B: Osmosis  C: Diffusion  D: Facilitated diffusion
ANS: C

Q : Which of the following describes a series circuit?
A: Components share the same voltage  B: Components are connected end-to-end and share the same current  C: Each component has its own independent switch  D: Current divides at each junction
ANS: B

Q : An ecological term for the role an organism plays in its ecosystem is its
A: Habitat  B: Niche  C: Community  D: Population
ANS: B

Q : Neutralisation of an acid with a base produces
A: A gas and water  B: Salt and water  C: Salt and acid  D: Base and water
ANS: B

Q : Which of the following is a renewable energy source?
A: Coal  B: Natural gas  C: Solar energy  D: Nuclear energy
ANS: C
