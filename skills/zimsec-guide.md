# ZIMSEC Exam Tips & Strategy

## General Tips
- Always read the question TWICE before answering
- Show all working — partial marks are awarded even for wrong final answers
- Write legibly and number your answers clearly
- Manage your time: allocate marks per minute (e.g. 2 mark question = 2 minutes max)
- Attempt every question — blank answers score zero

## Mathematics
- Write given, required, working, answer structure
- Always include units in your final answer
- Double-check arithmetic: a calculation error costs all marks
- For geometry: draw and label diagrams clearly
- Learn your log tables and how to use them

## Sciences
- Use scientific notation where appropriate
- Label all diagrams with arrows and proper names
- State assumptions in Physics questions
- Know your practical procedures — questions test understanding of method
- Memorise key definitions word-for-word

## English
- Plan your essay before writing (5 mins)
- Use varied vocabulary and sentence structure
- Always proofread your composition
- In comprehension: use evidence from the passage, don't give your own opinion unless asked
- Summary: only use what is in the passage, write in your own words

## Accounts
- Balance every account — a trial balance that doesn't balance loses marks
- Show workings for depreciation, accruals, prepayments
- Ledger headings must include account name, Dr/Cr, period

## Revision Strategy
- Past papers are the best revision tool — do at least 5 per subject
- Mark your own papers using mark schemes
- Focus on topics you consistently get wrong
- Revise 3 days before exam, not the night before
