# ZIMSEC Project Writing Template (Step-by-Step)

> **AI Guide:** When generating a ZIMSEC project, follow all 6 stages below in order.
> Use the student's topic to fill in each section with relevant, subject-appropriate content.
> Keep language simple and clear — suitable for O-Level or A-Level students in Zimbabwe.
> Replace all placeholders like `[School Name]` with realistic or student-supplied details.

---

## 📍 Stage 1: Problem Identification

### Project Title
Give your project a clear name that reflects the problem and proposed solution.

**Example:** Boosting Sales at Our School Tuckshop

---

### Objectives
State what you want to achieve. Use numbering.

**Example:**
1. To find out why students are not buying from the tuckshop
2. To come up with ways to attract more customers
3. To help the tuckshop increase its daily sales

---

### Problem Description
Write a paragraph describing:
- The problem
- Where it's happening
- Who is affected

**Example:**
At [School Name], the school tuckshop is facing low sales. Many students now prefer buying food from outside. This affects the tuckshop workers and school income.

---

### Statement of Intent
Write a short paragraph about what you intend to do to solve the problem.

**Example:**
I plan to design a colourful flier that will advertise the tuckshop and its products to attract more students.

---

### Design Specifications
List all materials you need for your solution.

**Example:**
- A smartphone
- PixelLab app
- Photos of products
- Colour printer
- A4 paper

---

## 📍 Stage 2: Investigation of Related Ideas

### 1. Existing Solutions
List solutions that other people or organizations have used for the same or similar problem.

**Example:**
- Giving discounts
- Using fliers
- Creating social media pages
- Adding new products

---

### 2. Advantages and Disadvantages
For each solution, say what is good (advantages) and what is not good (disadvantages).

**Example:**
- **Fliers:** Cheap and colourful, but some people may ignore them.
- **Discounts:** Encourage buying, but reduce profits.

---

### 3. Recommendations
Suggest how existing solutions can be improved.

**Example:**
Combine digital sharing with fliers to increase awareness.

---

### 4. Evidence
Mention how others used these solutions successfully.

**Example:**
A nearby school used WhatsApp to share tuckshop promotions and saw an increase in sales.

---

## 📍 Stage 3: Generation of Ideas

Come up with new ideas of your own and improve on existing ones. For each idea, give advantages and disadvantages.

**Example:**

- **New Idea:** Hold a "Snack of the Week" contest
- **Improved Idea:** Combine WhatsApp updates with colourful fliers
- **Advantages:** Catches attention, reaches more people
- **Disadvantages:** Needs regular updates and creativity

---

## 📍 Stage 4: Development of Idea

Choose the best solution from Stage 3, refine or improve it, and list its advantages and disadvantages.

**Example:**
I chose to design and print a flier, and share it online too. I improved it by adding a limited-time offer.

- **Advantages:** Eye-catching, fast sharing
- **Disadvantages:** Needs regular promotion and printing

---

## 📍 Stage 5: Presentation of Results

Present your final solution using any creative method such as:
- Digital flier
- Poster
- Audio jingle
- Video ad

**Example:**
I designed a colourful flier using PixelLab and printed it for the school noticeboard. I also shared it on the school WhatsApp group.

---

## 📍 Stage 6: Evaluation and Recommendations

Say what you achieved, mention challenges you faced, and give suggestions for future improvement.

**Example:**
I managed to create a useful flier that was shared around school. A challenge I faced was getting clear product photos.

**Recommendations:**
- Use student-designed ads regularly
- Add new affordable snacks
- Update fliers every term
